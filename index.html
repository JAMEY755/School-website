About Us

Where Education Meets Purpose & Excellence

Yope International High School is a premier mixed day and boarding secondary school located near Nesitu (close to Mutala) outside of Juba, South Sudan. Positioned directly along the prominent Juba-Nimule Highway, our campus offers a peaceful, spacious, and conducive environment ideal for dedicated study.

We believe that modern education should nurture both academic brilliance and real-world resilience. From Senior One (S1) through Senior Four (S4), our students receive rigorous intellectual preparation complemented by active hands-on agricultural programs and vibrant sports activities.
